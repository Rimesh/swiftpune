// ls ./programs

Less talk. Here's the work.

No roadmap theatre, no vague mission statements — three things we run on repeat, each with the full log of every one we've actually held. Talks for working developers, Swift taken onto campuses, and a monthly brunch where nobody gives a talk at all.

Meetups Talks for people already shipping. Half a weekend morning at a host company's office. Three 20-minute talks, a hands-on lab, every talk recorded. 2 editions · latest Mar 2026 Every edition
Student outreach Swift, taught on campus. We run the session inside the university, on their timetable, for students who haven't opened Xcode yet. 2 sessions · latest Jul 2026 Every session
Casuals Brunch, once a month. No talks, no agenda. A long table at a Pune restaurant, plus the WWDC keynote on a big screen every June. 2 casuals · latest Sep 2026 Every casual

// core_team

The core team.

No titles between us, no permanent assignments. Different companies, different specialties, one shared instinct: whoever has a free weekend grabs whatever the next meetup needs, then hands it back when it's done. This month's venue scout is next month's name-tag printer. Who's bringing the projector adapter is, as always, a separate conversation.

// sponsors

Supported by people who care.

Venues, food, name tags, the small AV bits — every meetup runs on someone's generosity. Hosts and sponsors get a stage minute at the event they back and a real thank-you from the room. Two companies have made it possible so far; the next slot should be yours.

notes/to_future_hosts.swift

A meetup needs almost nothing — a room for 75,

a projector, a few pots of coffee, three hours

of a weekend morning. We can't really sell

anyone on this. There's nothing to buy.

Past hosts have offered what they already had:

the conference room that was empty that morning,

the coffee budget no one was watching. The

talks were ours; the chairs were theirs.

That was the whole deal.

If you'd like to make a morning happen, write

to us. We'll shape it around whatever you can

offer. No tier list. No deck. No ask beyond

the room.

— the SwiftPune crew
