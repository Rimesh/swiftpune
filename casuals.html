// program.casuals

No talks. Just brunch.

Once a month we take a long table at a restaurant in Pune and spend a weekend brunch together. No agenda beyond meeting each other: talk about what you're building, hear what everyone else is up to, and put faces to the names in the group chat.

Join the next one on WhatsApp The WWDC watch party

  • // when

    Once a month, over brunch.

    A weekend brunch at a restaurant somewhere in Pune. The place and time go up in the WhatsApp group first.

  • // agenda

    Meet people. That's it.

    No talks and no slides. Tell people what you're building, hear what they are, and get to know the community.

  • // the bill

    We split it.

    There's no ticket. Everyone pays their share of the bill at the table.

  • // who

    Students welcome.

    Working developers, designers, students, the curious. You don't need to have come to a meetup first.

// once_a_year

One night a year, we just watch.

The one casual with a screen in it. Every June the community gets in one room for the WWDC keynote and reacts to it together, which is still the best way to watch it.

// latest night — WWDC26

The community's first proper party.

For WWDC26 we took over The Daily All Day in Pune, put the keynote on the big screen, and spent the evening reacting to every announcement in a room full of people who care as much as you do.

  • Where The Daily All Day, Pune
  • When Keynote night, June 2026
