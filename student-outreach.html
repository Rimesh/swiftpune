// program.student_outreach

Swift, taken to campus.

Most students won't travel across the city for a weekend meetup, so we go to them instead. Faculty book the hall, we bring the people who build on Apple's platforms for a living, and the session runs for whoever turns up — mostly people who haven't opened Xcode yet.
